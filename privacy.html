Privacy Policy

We keep things simple — and private. Here's exactly how we handle your data.

Last Updated: May 2025
Short version: Make My Doc processes all files entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, never stored, and never shared. We do use cookies for ads and analytics — details below.

📄 1. Who We Are

Make My Doc ("we", "us", "our") is a free online toolkit for working with PDF and image files. We are operated by Sitizen. Our website is accessible at makemydoc.in (or the domain you are currently viewing).

For privacy-related questions, you can contact us at: sitizentechnologies@gmail.com

🔒 2. Your Files — We Never See Them

All file processing on Make My Doc happens entirely within your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. This means:

  • Your files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Your files are never stored on any server or cloud.
  • Your files are never transmitted over the internet.
  • Once you close the browser tab, all data is gone.

We have no technical ability to access, view, or recover any file you process on this site.

📊 3. Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit Make My Doc, we (and our third-party partners) may automatically collect certain non-personal information, including:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, tools used, time on site, referring URL.
  • Device data: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, language.
  • IP address: Used for approximate geographic analytics (country/city level). We do not store full IP addresses.

This data is collected via cookies and analytics scripts (see Section 5). It is used solely to understand how the site is used and to improve our services.

🍪 4. Cookies

We use cookies — small text files stored in your browser — for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies: To understand visitor behaviour and improve the site (e.g. Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies: To display relevant ads via Google AdSense. These cookies track interests across sites to show personalised ads.
  • Preference cookies: To remember settings like your selected tool category.

You can control or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the site function.

📢 5. Advertising — Google AdSense

Make My Doc uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

🔗 6. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — advertising. Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

Make My Doc is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

🌍 8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your data.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (CCPA).
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.

Since we do not collect personal data directly (beyond anonymous analytics), most of these rights apply to data held by our third-party partners (Google). To exercise rights regarding Google's data, use the links in Section 5.

For any other privacy requests, email us at sitizentechnologies@gmail.com.

🔄 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

✉️ 10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:
